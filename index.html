BEFORE WE START

A few practical questions.

Understand the next step before sharing your project.

Can you work on my existing WordPress website?

Yes. Share the affected URL, what you want to change and any recent issues. I review the existing setup before recommending a repair, an extension or a larger change. Explore WordPress troubleshooting or custom development.

Do you work with international clients and agencies?

I work remotely from Yamuna Nagar, India. We can discuss your brief by email and arrange a call at a mutually suitable time. Include your time zone and any meeting constraints so we can agree on a workable schedule.

How do you estimate the cost and timeline?

The estimate depends on the current website, the required behaviour and the scope of testing. Send your URL, project goal and preferred deadline. We agree on the deliverables and estimate before work begins; unfamiliar issues may need an initial investigation.

What should I send with my first enquiry?

Your website URL, a short description of the issue or goal, and your preferred timeline are enough to start. Screenshots and steps to reproduce a problem help. Please do not send passwords or sensitive customer data; access can be arranged after we discuss the scope.
