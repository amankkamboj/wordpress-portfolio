Working with restricted access

The source website was accessible through Drupal administration, without database or filesystem access. That constraint shaped the migration: the content and structure needed careful review rather than a direct database transfer.

What I rebuilt

I reconstructed the website in WordPress, migrating page content and linked files while retaining the source design and content relationships. The work included page hierarchy and intermediate navigation pages that connected sections.

Client review

The delivery conversation records content and file migration work in July 2024 and an approved paid milestone. On 18 November, the client explicitly confirmed that the website was approved.

An approved rebuild with a later launch

The client planned to go live in a later quarter and expected another synchronization before launch. This case therefore documents the approved reconstruction, without claiming a verified production launch, exact page count or preserved search rankings.
