The strongest differentiator is working directly with a developer who can explain and extend an existing system. ## Current demand and search guidance

The [public Upwork WooCommerce job sample](https://www.upwork.com/freelance-jobs/woocommerce/) currently includes plugin development, initial setup with ongoing support, store management, technical support and mobile styling fixes. This is a directional sample of buyer requests, not keyword-volume research or a forecast of leads. It supports focusing on existing-store improvements, custom functionality and reliable ongoing help.

[WooCommerce's August 2026 release notes](https://woocommerce.com/posts/woocommerce-11-0-update/) emphasize product/order-screen performance and analytics reliability. The existing performance, checkout and integration offer is aligned with these operational concerns. Do not add HPOS, Checkout Blocks, subscriptions or other specialist compatibility promises merely because they are current; first provide actual experience and test evidence. The service already correctly distinguishes classic and block-based checkout.

[Google's people-first guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) favors original experience, descriptive headings and trustworthy evidence. It sets no preferred word count. Shortening repetitive paragraphs is reasonable when it improves decisions; adding length just for SEO is not.

[Google's AI-search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) says normal SEO remains applicable and no special AI files or schema are required. Clear textual answers, useful internal links and real supporting imagery are the practical priorities. No AI-search appearance is guaranteed.

Avoid fake stars, unsupported revenue claims, location-doorway pages and purchased ranking links. [Google's spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) address keyword stuffing, doorway abuse, link spam and scaled low-value content. The current services cover distinct needs; retain that distinction. [Review-snippet guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/review-snippet) is not a reason to add self-promotional rating markup to this portfolio.

## Efficient next steps

1. **Verify enquiry delivery and measurement.** Send a clearly labelled test from the public homepage, check receipt/reply-to and confirm a consented generate_lead in GA4. Set it as a key event in the property. Review optional enhanced-measurement URL collection/redaction. Do not infer that a successful frontend message proves inbox delivery.
2. **Strengthen three accepted projects.** For each, supply an anonymizable screenshot or demonstration, the exact change you implemented, tests performed, and final feedback/acceptance. Include quantitative outcomes only with a reproducible baseline and date. Do not share passwords or private customer records.
3. **Define buying choices.** Confirm whether you offer small fixes, scoped custom development and recurring support; provide actual scope, deliverables, exclusions, handover and availability. Publish pricing or response times only if they reflect commitments you can maintain. No invented package minimums or turnaround promises.
4. **Publish one useful technical story at a time.** Candidate topics: manufacturer/model product filters; WooCommerce tax integration test checks; WC Vendors purchased-versus-sold navigation. Use real lessons and links to the relevant service/case study. Avoid mass-produced articles or identical city pages.
5. **Use existing distribution.** Share a problem/solution example on LinkedIn with a campaign-tagged link to its case study. Add the portfolio to relevant public professional profiles. Seek genuine client recommendations with permission. Track enquiries rather than accumulating low-quality backlinks.
6. **Review outcomes on a rolling 28-day basis.** Compare Search Console queries and landing pages, GA4 session source/medium, accepted enquiries and qualified replies. With a small sample, record trends without declaring statistical certainty. Current data cannot establish which offer produces paying clients.
