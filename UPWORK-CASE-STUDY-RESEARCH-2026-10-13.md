# Additional Upwork case-study research
Reviewed: 13 October 2026

## Research scope
Screened 67 contracts and selected 32 for deeper investigation. Retrieved the complete available message history for 31 selected contracts; one selected contract had no accessible room. Compared opportunities with the existing portfolio to avoid repeating its WooCommerce, malware, performance, Divi, affiliate, enquiry, and sitemap stories.

This is a research shortlist, not a verification of current production websites. Client acceptance and paid milestones support delivery; they do not establish conversion, revenue, traffic, security, or performance improvements. Image counts refer to image attachment metadata in the conversations, not visually verified portfolio screenshots. This report intentionally omits credentials, private message exports, personal records, and signed attachment URLs.

## Six strongest additional stories

### 1. Scored WordPress cybersecurity assessment
Upwork contract: WordPress-Based Cybersecurity Assessment Tool.
Suggested title: Building a scored WordPress assessment with saved results and CRM handoff.
- Problem: Turn a client-defined assessment and scoring model into an interactive WordPress tool.
- Contribution: Custom plugin/shortcode, assessment flow, overall and domain scoring, stored submissions, recommendations, and CRM handoff work.
- Evidence: On 9 July 2026, the client explicitly confirmed checking the tool, logic, and scoring. Complete-tool and final-handoff milestones were paid.
- Visual leads: 13 image attachments across the conversation. Suitable captures would show the questionnaire, anonymized results, and admin submission/sync interface.
- Boundaries: Scoring methodology came from the client. This was not a cybersecurity certification or independent security audit. Zoho receipt and email cadence execution were not independently verified. Later revision requests exist; avoid claiming every revision was verified complete. Unfunded website-rebuild milestones are excluded.
- Portfolio value: Strong custom WordPress development example with concrete application logic.

### 2. Drupal-to-WordPress migration with restricted source access
Upwork contract: Need to copy a drupal website to WordPress.
Suggested title: Rebuilding a Drupal content hierarchy in WordPress with admin-only source access.
- Problem: Reproduce design, content, page hierarchy, and linked files without source database or filesystem access.
- Contribution: WordPress reconstruction and content/file migration, including hierarchy and intermediate navigation pages.
- Evidence: Delivery discussion in July 2024, approved paid milestone, and explicit client confirmation on 18 November 2024 that the website was approved.
- Visual leads: 2 image attachments. Seek source/destination page comparisons, hierarchy, and migrated file links.
- Boundaries: Client deferred going live until a later quarter and anticipated another synchronization. Describe an approved rebuild, not a verified production launch. Do not invent migrated page counts or automated migration capabilities.
- Portfolio value: Distinct migration story with a meaningful technical constraint.

### 3. Searchable business directory using Gravity Forms and GravityView
Upwork contract: Business Directory using GravityForm/GravityKit/GravityView built needed.
Suggested title: Turning business registrations into searchable directory listings.
- Problem: Existing registrations needed useful visitor-facing listings and category discovery.
- Contribution: Registration-field mapping, category searches/lists, category counts, and compact listing cards.
- Evidence: On 23 May 2025, the client confirmed the result looked good and said they were ending the contract.
- Visual leads: 17 image attachments. Prioritize category filtering, listing cards, and registration-to-listing field mapping.
- Boundaries: The client already had the registration form and listing foundation. Do not attribute the entire platform to this engagement. A promised five-star review is not proof of an actual rating. Later minor revision/support discussions exist.
- Portfolio value: Strong fit for directory, Gravity Forms, and custom workflow services.

### 4. Consulting website migration and WordPress rebuild
Upwork contract: Expert Website Migration Specialist Needed.
Suggested title: Rebuilding a consulting website in WordPress while preserving its content and structure.
- Problem: Move an existing .NET website experience to a new domain/host while retaining content and design.
- Contribution: WordPress setup, theme/staging work, rebuilt pages/content, domain/contact updates, navigation/breadcrumb fixes, and handover.
- Evidence: Three paid milestones cover setup, content/page migration, and final testing/go-live. On 11 June 2025, the client confirmed seeing all requested changes completed and arranged knowledge transfer.
- Visual leads: 16 image attachments. Look for before/after pages, navigation, and responsive layouts.
- Boundaries: This was a WordPress rebuild, not simply a .NET server migration. Do not claim zero downtime, preserved rankings, or measured speed improvements.
- Portfolio value: End-to-end delivery story spanning implementation and client handover.

### 5. Recruitment questionnaire and reporting workflow
Upwork contract: Gravity Forms Candidate Screening Questionnaire.
Suggested title: Building reusable recruitment questionnaires with consolidated reporting.
- Problem: Replace a fragmented questionnaire process with reusable campaign forms and manageable response exports.
- Contribution: Master/campaign questionnaires, notification configuration, role-controlled administration, response consolidation/export plugin work, and handover documentation.
- Evidence: On 14 July 2026, the client reported four successful submission tests and confirmed receipt of a test notification. On 21 July, they reported extracting a consolidated CSV of live responses. On 24 July, they accepted the work and confirmed reviewing delivered source files. A later duplicated-form acknowledgement issue received a specific successful retest on 23 September.
- Visual leads: 38 image attachments across the mixed engagement, including other work; not all belong to recruitment. Use empty forms, fictional responses, and sanitized admin screens.
- Boundaries: Keep separate from the existing companionship enquiry case. Bulk invitations, reminders, invitation tracking, unique candidate links, ATS integration, and PDF reports were excluded. Do not claim reliable server-triggered scheduling: the conversation does not establish installation of a hosting-level cron. External advice contributed to the consolidation design.
- Portfolio value: A detailed operations/workflow story with direct testing evidence.

### 6. WordPress critical-error recovery
Upwork contract: Wordpress website down - need to fix critical error.
Suggested title: Restoring a WordPress site by isolating a conflicting plugin.
- Problem: A plugin conflict caused a critical error and blocked normal site use.
- Contribution: Isolated and disabled the conflicting plugin to restore access.
- Evidence: Client messages on 9 September 2023 confirmed that the site was working and accepted the immediate recovery.
- Visual leads: 4 image attachments. A sanitized error/recovery comparison would suit a short case study.
- Boundaries: Evidence supports immediate restoration, not a permanent repair to the underlying plugin. Later discussion mentions further investigation without documented final compatibility repair.
- Portfolio value: Concise troubleshooting story, separate from malware removal.

## Do not present as completed success stories yet
- GeneratePress/Figma implementation: cancelled milestone and unfinished revisions.
- LiteSpeed HTTPS repair: initial acceptance followed by recurrence of a blank screen; no documented final resolution.
- Service marketplace build: extensive QA and revision work, without clear final launch signoff.
- Nonprofit platform: scope and gateway dependencies remained open.
- CMS/intranet rebuild: brief conversation and review pending.
- Broad CRM integrations: implementation reports mixed with unresolved API issues; some integrations were performed by the client.
- General maintenance contracts with only recruitment, requirements, off-platform work, or recurring unresolved problems.
- Cancelled/unfunded catalog, hotel-booking, and payment-gateway proposals.

## Recommended next portfolio batch
Start with the assessment, Drupal migration, and business directory. They add distinct service coverage and have specific client acceptance evidence. Follow with the consulting rebuild and recruitment workflow. Use the critical-error recovery as a short technical case.

For each page, use problem, constraints, owned contribution, verified delivery, and handover sections. Add annotated, sanitized screenshots after visual review. Present measured business outcomes only if analytics or other supporting records can be obtained. Where the production domain or its ownership is uncertain, do not guess a website URL or label its current appearance as the delivered version.
